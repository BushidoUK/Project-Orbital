### **ORB Takedowns**

This file tracks court-authorised operations and disruption actions executed by law enforcement to dismantle operational relay box (ORB) networks utilized by state-sponsored actors.

| Date | Target ORB Network | Associated Threat Actor | Takedown Summary & Technical Details | 
| --- | --- | --- | --- | 
| **Jan 2024** | **KV Botnet** | **Volt Typhoon** (VANGUARD PANDA) | FBI/DOJ operation wiped the botnet malware from infected Cisco and Netgear SOHO (Small Office/Home Office) routers. The operation deleted the malware and cut communication with the C2 (command-and-control) infrastructure, neutralizing the proxy mesh used to hide attacks on critical infrastructure. | 
| **Sep 2024** | **Sparrow** | **Flax Typhoon** (ETHEREAL PANDA, Integrity Technology Group) | FBI and international partners seized C2 servers and disrupted a massive 260,000-device botnet running specialized IoT malware. During the course of the operation, there was an attempt to interfere with the FBI’s remediation efforts through a distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attack targeting the operational infrastructure that the FBI was utilizing to effectuate the court’s orders. | 
| **Aug 2026** | **QTRouter** | **QTFY** (PRC state-sponsored group employed by Nanjing Xinjiuwei Network Technology Co.; services used by China's MSS and PLA) | **Court-authorized domain seizures:** The DOJ and FBI seized domain infrastructure hard-coded into the QScan and QTRouter malware used for authentication and C2. This rendered the operational relay box (ORB) network and targeting platform inoperable. |
