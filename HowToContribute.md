## How To Contribute to Project ORBITAL

- It is amazing that members of the community want to lend their support to this project and help build up **Project ORBITAL**.
- Please follow simple the guidelines below if you wish to contribute.
- If you have ideas, feel free to create an issue rather than a pull request. 

> [!IMPORTANT]
> To contribute to this repository, researchers must follow the How to Contribute guide, otherwise pull requests will be closed without merging them.

### Adding New Info
- Make sure to follow the markdown .md table format.
- Please cite your sources of information for where it was reported that this vulnerability was exploited by a ransomware adversary.
- Please do not submit ORB updates to this repository if there are no public sources to support your submission.
- Please use official & reputable sources as it is important that others can independently verify your claims.
- The quality of the evidence provided shall be judged during a review of the pull request - it may be closed if the evidence is insufficient to support your commit.
